A QUICK RECIPE FOR TEAMWORK

Pick it up. Get cooking.

Player one

W A S D Move and turn

E Pick up, place, combine, or serve

Space Chop ingredients or wash plates

Tab Switch chefs in teammate mode

Player two

↑ ↓ ← → Move and turn

Enter Pick up or place

Shift Chop ingredients or wash plates

P Pause / resume

You can also click a station. Your chef will walk over and interact once. Chopping continues until finished; moving interrupts it.

Place ingredients on a board before chopping. Pots and pans cook automatically, so collect food before it burns. Combine prepared ingredients on a counter or plate, then bring the finished dish to the serving station.

Layouts and timing are simplified. See the project README for references, adaptations, and research interfaces.
